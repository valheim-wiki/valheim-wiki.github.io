Prepare before you leave camp

  1. Know what you will fight

    Check attacks, damage types and star levels before choosing a weapon. The Valheim Troll guide brings its weaknesses and drops together.

  2. Choose food and gear together

    Try equipment quality and three foods before spending materials. The Valheim food and gear planner shows the combined health, stamina and armour of your choices.

  3. Gather for the whole build

    Add the pieces and quantities you want to make. The Valheim build cost calculator combines their materials into one gathering list.
